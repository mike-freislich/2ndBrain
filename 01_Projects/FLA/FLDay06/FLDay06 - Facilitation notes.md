## Stage one - Mike

### 1. Mathias Tölken - From Excel Chaos to Flow: Two Years of Scaling Flight Level 2 at BASF with Businessmap

#### Version A

Next on stage one is Mathias Tölken, Flight Levels Professional®. Mathias will take us into BASF Tamol, where nearly 150 people moved from Excel-based coordination chaos to a working Flight Level 2 system in Businessmap. You’ll hear what changed in daily work, how flow became visible, and what measurable impact this had on WIP, cycle time, maintenance cost, and product quality.

#### Version B

Our first talk on stage one comes from Mathias Tölken, Flight Levels Professional®. This is a real industrial case from BASF Tamol: many products, multiple plants, lots of dependencies, and Excel no longer doing the job. Mathias will show how Flight Level 2 helped turn hidden work into visible flow — and what happened when coordination became part of everyday work.

### 2. Peter Faulks - Take-off without permission: Towards organisational agility with Flight Levels.

#### Version A

Next up is Peter Faulks. His talk is about starting a Flight Levels journey without perfect conditions, without waiting for official permission, and without pretending that organizational change is clean and easy. Peter will share how value was demonstrated step by step in a public organization, and how that created pull for broader change.

#### Version B

Now we welcome Peter Faulks to stage one. This session is for everyone who has ever thought, “We don’t have the sponsorship, the timing, or the ideal setup yet.” Peter will talk about taking off anyway — using Flight Levels to create visible value first, and letting momentum grow from there.

### 3. Cameron Leask - One Golden Thread - From Boardroom to Building Site across multiple organisations

#### Version A

Next on stage one is Cameron Leask. Cameron will share a story about connecting all three Flight Levels across multiple organizations — from boardroom strategy to work happening on building sites. This is about creating one golden thread through a complex system, so strategy, coordination, and delivery stop living in separate worlds.

#### Version B

Our next speaker is Cameron Leask. His talk is about what happens when Flight Levels are not used as isolated boards, but as a connected system across organizational boundaries. From strategic decisions to operational delivery, Cameron will show how one golden thread can make work, dependencies, and outcomes visible across the whole system.

### 4. Victor Fairén - From optimizing a team to redesigning how an entire organization flows

#### Version A

Next up is Victor Fairén. Victor will talk about moving beyond team-level optimization and gradually redesigning how work flows through an organization. His case starts at the operational level and grows toward coordination across teams, products, and strategic decisions — with a very practical message: transformation is not about installing a framework, it’s about evolving the system.

#### Version B

Now we welcome Victor Fairén to stage one. This talk is about a familiar trap: improving teams while the organization around them still blocks flow. Victor will share how small, practical steps helped expand from local improvement to broader coordination — and which decisions really mattered along the way.

### 5. Troy Magennis - Dependency Management in Flight Levels Systems - Now with Added AI

#### Version A

Next on stage one is Troy Magennis. Troy will look at dependency management in Flight Levels systems — and what changes when AI engineering enters the picture. The key question is: if AI changes some constraints, what new dependencies appear, and how can Flight Levels help us see and manage them before they slow down value delivery?

#### Version B

Our next speaker is Troy Magennis. This session connects two very current topics: dependencies and AI. Troy will explore what happens when AI changes the way work is engineered, but not the need for coordination. Because even with AI in the system, dependencies do not magically disappear — they just get more interesting.

### 6. Craeg Strong - Improving System Delivery at the nation's largest public Housing Authority with Flight Levels

#### Version A

Our final talk on stage one is from Craeg Strong, Flight Levels Professional®. Craeg will take us into the New York City Housing Authority, the largest public housing authority in North America. This is a story about a huge backlog, urgent operational pressure, and how Flight Levels helped create transparency, prioritization, and better system delivery in a highly constrained environment.

#### Version B

Closing stage one, we have Craeg Strong, Flight Levels Professional®. This is a big public-sector case with real pressure behind it: too much demand, too much backlog, and not enough clarity across the system. Craeg will show how Flight Levels helped NYCHA move from permanent triage toward better flow and delivery capability.
