> **develop a substantially superior product that can serve a wider range of ages, learner profiles, and languages that enables 60%+ of learners to achieve fluent or emergent fluent by the end of Standard 4**
> 
> 1. **Substantially superior product**
> 	1. Curriculum coverage

> 		2. > 		3. Our [Activity Catalog](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1-wAorVhGyh-1hV1aHnjdj0W1d2H0iKw2k25PaEnC4GA/edit?gid=0#gid=0) for numeracy and literacy, defines the activities intended to teach these skills to learners. This catalog is a work in process.
