# FL2D Workshop Meeting with Schlueter

**3. Global Footprint**

Schluter Systems has a massive international footprint with close to 1,000 employees globally.

- **Headquarters:** The global headquarters is located in Iserlohn, Germany.
- **North American Presence:** The company has a massive foothold in the US and Canada, with primary facilities in Plattsburgh, New York; Reno, Nevada; Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas; and Montreal, Quebec.  

**4. Target Audience and Industry Impact**

Schluter serves multiple segments of the construction industry, including tile contractors, general builders, architects, specifiers, and distributors. Because their systems have made complex tile jobs more accessible, their products are also highly popular among DIY homeowners.  

The company is heavily invested in industry education. They run highly regarded workshops and training programs worldwide to teach contractors how to properly install their systems to industry standards. Furthermore, Schluter strictly tests its products through independent organizations like the Tile Council of North America (TCNA) to ensure compliance and reliability.

In summary, Schluter Systems is the industry standard-bearer for modern tile installation, transitioning the trade away from traditional, labor-intensive mortar bed methods into highly engineered, specialized systems that guarantee a waterproof and crack-free finish.

**Final Decisions to Make**

By the end of the 45 minutes, you need definitive answers on:

1. **The Cohort Structure:** Are you running ONE group of 18 people, or TWO smaller groups?
2. **The Schedule:** Are they officially locked in for half-days starting in May?
3. **The Pricing Agreement:** Does Rebecca understand and agree that if they choose to split the group, a new quote will be generated reflecting the doubled facilitation time? (Keep your spreadsheet handy—you are currently making €11,309 profit on the single 18-pax session. Don't compromise your margin for their operational convenience).
4. **Next Step Ownership:** Who is sending what, and by when? (e.g., "I will send a revised proposal based on this call by tomorrow, and Ashley will provide potential dates").
